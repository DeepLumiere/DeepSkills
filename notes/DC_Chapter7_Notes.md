# Chapter 7 — Transmission Media

## 1. Guided Media vs Unguided Media

| Media Type | Physical Construction | Bandwidth / Speed | Immunity to EMI | Cost | Source Tag |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |

| **Twisted-Pair (UTP/STP)** | Twisted copper pairs | Moderate (up to 10 Gbps) | Low to Moderate | Low | [Source: Ch 7, Slide 4] |

| **Coaxial Cable** | Central conductor, insulator, metallic shield | High (up to 500 Mbps) | Good | Moderate | [Source: Ch 7, Slide 8] |

| **Fiber-Optic Cable** | Glass core, cladding, jacket | Extremely High (Tbps) | Complete (Immune to EMI) | High | [Source: Ch 7, Slide 12] |
