# Chapter 8 — Switching (Circuit, Packet, & Datagram Networks)

## 1. Circuit Switching vs Packet Switching

| Feature | Circuit Switching | Packet Switching (Datagram) | Virtual Circuit Packet Switching | Source Tag |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |

| **Dedicated Path** | Yes (Physical circuit set up) | No (Packets routed independently) | Yes (Logical virtual circuit path) | [Source: Ch 8, Slide 4] |

| **Resource Reservation** | Reserved upfront | On-demand (Store-and-forward) | Reserved along virtual path | [Source: Ch 8, Slide 8] |

| **Delay** | Setup delay, low transmission delay | Packet queuing delay | Setup delay + packet delay | [Source: Ch 8, Slide 15] |
